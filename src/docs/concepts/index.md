# Concepts

The ideas you work with when building an interface with Auxior.

- [Layout](layout.md) explains how containers decide where
  each widget goes and how large it is.
- [Text and Unicode](text.md) covers display width, wide
  characters and wrapping.
- [Keys, focus and the mouse](input-and-focus.md) shows how to
  make an app respond to the user.
- [Scrolling](scrolling.md) explains content taller than its
  space.
- [Writing your own widgets](custom-widgets.md) walks through
  implementing [`Widget`](crate::Widget).
